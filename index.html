What the rules mean

  1. 1Top watch: a completed quarter's revision percentage is below the previous quarter's. Two consecutive weaker quarters, plus positive aligned SPX returns in both, form this tool's trigger. This needs at least three completed-quarter readings.
  2. 2Bottom watch: three consecutive negative weekly changes get progressively less negative. Four consecutive weekly observations of the same fixed-year EPS are required. This is a transparent approximation, not Nick's exact rule.
  3. 3Missing data, a new forecast year, or a missed week breaks the comparison. Falling EPS is not required for a top watch. Falling EPS can coexist with an easing-cuts bottom watch.

The reported-current-quarter revision series and fixed-year weekly series are separate proxies. Neither reproduces Nick's blended quarterly/annual forward estimate score. No audited backtest or accuracy claim is provided.

Early-reporters barometer unavailableFactSet's earnings surprises are actual EPS versus estimates, not post-report estimate revisions for an off-calendar cohort. The tool does not substitute surprise percentages for that missing metric.
